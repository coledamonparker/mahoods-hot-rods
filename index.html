FAMILY OWNED SINCE 1980

Mark & Jake Mahood — Two Generations of Anaheim Craftsmanship

Founded by Mark Mahood in 1980, Mahood's Collision & Hot Rods has spent over four decades repairing daily drivers and painting some of the finest classic cars, Porsches, and street rods in the country.

Mark is an AMBR (America's Most Beautiful Roadster) Award winner who has worked alongside Boyd Coddington, Chip Foose, and Fat Jack. Today, Mark and his son Jake bring that exact same standard of craftsmanship to every collision repair, dent repair, and restoration that enters our Anaheim shop.
