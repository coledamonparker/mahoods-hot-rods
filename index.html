FAMILY OWNED SINCE 1980

Mark & Jake Mahood — Two Generations of Anaheim Craftsmanship

Founded by Mark Mahood in 1980 right out of school, Mahood's Collision & Hot Rods has spent more than four decades painting some of the finest street rods, classic Porsches, and muscle cars in the United States.

Mark has painted hundreds of show winners — earning the prestigious AMBR (America's Most Beautiful Roadster) Award and collaborating with icons including Boyd Coddington, Chip Foose, and Fat Jack. Today, Mark and his son Jake bring that exact same show-car eye for detail to every classic restoration and everyday collision repair that rolls into our Anaheim shop.
